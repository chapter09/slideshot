# SlideShot

Landing, support and privacy pages for SlideShot, an iPhone app that captures presentation slides,
straightens them and labels them on-device.

| Language | Home | Support | Privacy policy |
|---|---|---|---|
| English | https://www.haow.us/slideshot/ | [support.html](https://www.haow.us/slideshot/support.html) | [privacy.html](https://www.haow.us/slideshot/privacy.html) |
| 日本語 | https://www.haow.us/slideshot/ja/ | [support.html](https://www.haow.us/slideshot/ja/support.html) | [privacy.html](https://www.haow.us/slideshot/ja/privacy.html) |
| 简体中文 | https://www.haow.us/slideshot/zh-hans/ | [support.html](https://www.haow.us/slideshot/zh-hans/support.html) | [privacy.html](https://www.haow.us/slideshot/zh-hans/privacy.html) |
| 繁體中文 | https://www.haow.us/slideshot/zh-hant/ | [support.html](https://www.haow.us/slideshot/zh-hant/support.html) | [privacy.html](https://www.haow.us/slideshot/zh-hant/privacy.html) |
| 한국어 | https://www.haow.us/slideshot/ko/ | [support.html](https://www.haow.us/slideshot/ko/support.html) | [privacy.html](https://www.haow.us/slideshot/ko/privacy.html) |

Every page has a language menu at the top and `hreflang` links to its other versions. English is
the default, and the translated privacy policies say the English version applies if they differ.
The pages share `home.css` (landing pages) and `doc.css` (support and privacy). When you change a
page, change the same page in every language folder.

Found a problem or have a question? [Open an issue](https://github.com/chapter09/slideshot/issues/new).
