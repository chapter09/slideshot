SlideShot

Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

The short version

SlideShot collects no data. Everything you capture, the labels it creates and the notes you write stay on your iPhone. There is no account, no server and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Slide photos, corrected slide images, recognized text, talk labels (titles, speakers, conferences, dates) and your notes are stored only in SlideShot's storage on your iPhone. If your device backs up to iCloud, they are included in that backup like other app data, under Apple's privacy terms. Deleting the app deletes them.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only while you capture slides. Photos you choose to import are read through the system photo picker, which gives SlideShot only the photos you select. If you turn on "Also save to Photos", corrected slides are added to your photo library. SlideShot never reads your library otherwise.

Text recognition and Apple Intelligence

Slide text is recognized with Apple's on-device frameworks. On supported devices, labels are suggested by Apple Intelligence's on-device model. Nothing is sent to us or to any third party.

Sharing

Slides, PDFs and images leave your device only when you share them, through the iOS share sheet, to the destination you choose.

Analytics, ads and third parties

SlideShot contains no analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs, and makes no network requests.

Children

SlideShot does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
