What SlideShot does

  • Capture

    Every slide, captured automatically

    Point your iPhone at a projector screen or monitor. SlideShot finds the slide, waits until it's steady and captures it, skipping slides you already have.

  • Straighten

    Straight and flat, from any seat

    Perspective correction recovers each slide's true shape, even from off to the side, and auto-zoom uses the telephoto lens when your iPhone has one.

  • Auto labeling

    Filed by talk and conference

    SlideShot reads every slide and labels each talk with its title, speakers, conference and date, then groups talks into conferences. No typing.

  • Notes and search

    Find any slide later

    Add a short note to any slide, then search across titles, speakers, slide text and your notes.

  • Share

    A whole talk in one image

    Share a talk as one phone-width image, with or without your notes, or export it as a PDF.

  • Private

    Stays on your iPhone

    No account, no servers, no tracking. SlideShot collects no data, and your slides and notes stay on your device.
