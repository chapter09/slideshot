SlideShot: Talk Capture

Conference slides, organized

The fastest way to keep the slides from every talk you attend. Point your camera at any slide and SlideShot captures it, straightens it, reads it, and files it under the right talk, speaker and conference, all on your iPhone.

Sound familiar?

  • The angle problem

    Sat off to the side? Your slide photos come out skewed.

    SlideShot finds the slide and straightens it to its true shape, as if you had the original deck.

  • The camera roll problem

    500 photos later, which slide was from which talk?

    SlideShot reads every slide and files it under its talk, speaker and conference.

  • The memory problem

    A week later, what was that talk about?

    SlideShot keeps each slide's text and your notes together, and makes all of it searchable.

  • The sharing problem

    Sharing a talk shouldn't mean sending 40 photos.

    SlideShot shares a whole talk as one image or one PDF.

What SlideShot does

  • Capture

    Every slide, captured automatically

    Point your iPhone at a projector screen or monitor. SlideShot finds the slide, waits until it's steady and captures it, skipping slides you already have.

  • Straighten

    Straight and flat, from any seat

    Perspective correction recovers each slide's true shape, even from off to the side, and auto-zoom uses the telephoto lens when your iPhone has one.

  • Auto labeling

    Filed by talk and conference

    SlideShot reads every slide and labels each talk with its title, speakers, conference and date, then groups talks into conferences. No typing.

  • Notes and search

    Find any slide later

    Add a short note to any slide, then search across titles, speakers, slide text and your notes.

  • Share

    A whole talk in one image

    Share a talk as one phone-width image, with or without your notes, or export it as a PDF.

  • Private

    Stays on your iPhone

    No account, no servers, no tracking. SlideShot collects no data, and your slides and notes stay on your device.

Requires iOS 26 or later.
