Support

Contact

Open an issue on GitHub (a free GitHub account is needed). Include your iPhone model and iOS version, and a screenshot if something looks wrong. You can also browse existing issues.

The slide outline doesn't appear

Make sure the whole slide, all four corners, is in view. Very bright projectors in dark rooms work best once the outline turns green; tap the slide to focus and meter on it.

A slide came out cropped or skewed

Open the slide, tap the crop button and drag the four corners. Tap Detect to try automatic detection again.

Slides landed in the wrong talk

On a talk, long-press a slide and choose "Start New Talk Here", or use Select in the library to merge talks. Labels you edit by hand are never overwritten.

Auto-capture takes too many or too few photos

Turn auto-capture off with the A button on the camera screen to capture manually, or change it in Settings.

Privacy

Everything stays on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
